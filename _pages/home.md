I am a physicist working in **geophysical fluid dynamics**, currently a Research Fellow in the Vortex Dynamics Group at the University of St Andrews, working with Daphné Lemasquerier.

I am interested in **rotating and stratified flows**, especially coherent structures such as vortices, fronts and filaments, as well as Ekman layers, inertial oscillations, instabilities and convection. I combine **laboratory experiments, numerical modelling and theory** to understand the physical mechanisms behind how these structures form, evolve and interact.

My PhD (Université Grenoble Alpes, IGE & LEGI) looked at how wind and convection mix the upper ocean, and at the dynamics of turbulent Ekman layers. More on the [research](/research/) page.
